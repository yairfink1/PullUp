Elevate your pull-up training with The Amazing Pull Up!

Tired of having to manually tap your watch or losing count mid-set? The Amazing Pull Up uses high-frequency 100 Hz accelerometer motion processing to count your pull-ups automatically and accurately live on your wrist, saving you the burden of entering reps manually.

Whether you're doing "grease the groove" sets throughout the day or full calisthenics sessions, this app makes sure every single rep counts.

KEY HIGHLIGHTS & FEATURES:
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. AUTOMATIC & ACCURATE REP COUNTING
Advanced 100 Hz sensor technology automatically identifies each repetition and chin-over-bar inflection. It ignores setup and mounting shakes, so you can just jump on the bar and pull without pressing extra buttons.

2. TRACK YOUR PROGRESS
Effortlessly log every repetition you perform. Watch your daily and 7-day volume grow over time and see the physical results of your hard work.

3. 100% FREE TO USE
No paywalls, no subscriptions, and no trial expirations. A completely free tool built for the calisthenics and fitness community.

4. BUILD CONSISTENCY & GET STRONGER
Consistency is the #1 key to pull-up strength. The app provides clear daily feedback that motivates you to stay consistent with your training day after day, ensuring you steadily get stronger.

5. FLEXIBLE MODES: QUICK SETS OR STRUCTURED WORKOUTS
• Single Quick Set: Hop on the bar for a quick standalone set whenever you pass by—ideal for grease-the-groove training.
• Multi-Set Workout: Complete multiple sets back-to-back with dedicated rest timers between sets to keep your training structured.

6. SPURS YOU TO BEAT YOUR RECORDS
Tracks your all-time Personal Records across 3 categories:
• Best Single Set (Most reps in 1 set)
• Best Day (Highest total reps in a day)
• Best Week (Highest total reps in a week)
When you beat a personal record, the app congratulates you with a celebratory gold screen and celebratory vibration pulse!

7. MINIMALIST WIDGET GLANCE
Quickly check your current daily and weekly totals in a split second right from the Garmin glance carousel without even opening the full app.

8. GARMIN CONNECT FIT SYNC
Saves your sessions directly to Garmin Connect as Strength Training activities with developer rep fields visible in your Garmin Connect activity stats.
